About

I am a 4th-year Computer Engineering student currently completing an internship at EY within the Cyber Security area (CNS EW - TC). I participate in projects related to cybersecurity, ranging from regulatory compliance (GDPR, ISO, information security) to training in ethical hacking, pentesting and vulnerability analysis.


I am particularly interested in how technology can improve security, risk management and digital transformation. I am also motivated by participating in projects where technology brings real value to business through analysis, digital transformation and process optimization.


During my degree I have worked with programming languages such as Python, Java, C++, SQL, VHDL and MATLAB, as well as tools such as Power BI and Excel, also applying agile methodologies in different academic projects.


I lived for 7 years in the United Kingdom, which gave me fluency in English, a global perspective and the ability to adapt to different environments. I have also worked in sectors such as executive transportation (VTC) and hospitality, where I developed skills in customer service, incident resolution, leadership and time management.


I consider myself an analytical and proactive person with a strong willingness to keep learning and contributing within the fields of cybersecurity and technology consulting.
