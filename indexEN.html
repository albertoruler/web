About

I am a 4th-year Computer Engineering student and currently completing an internship at EY within the Cyber Security area (CNS EW - TC). I participate in cybersecurity-related projects, from regulatory compliance (GDPR, ISO, information security) to training in ethical hacking, pentesting and vulnerability analysis.

I am particularly interested in how technology can improve security, risk management and digital transformation. I am also motivated by participating in projects where technology provides value to the business through analysis, digital transformation and process optimization.

Throughout my studies I have worked with languages such as Python, Java, C++, SQL, VHDL and MATLAB, as well as tools like Power BI and Excel, also applying agile methodologies in various academic projects.

I lived for 7 years in the United Kingdom, which gave me fluency in English, a global mindset and adaptability to different environments. I have also worked in sectors such as executive transport (VTC) and hospitality, where I developed skills in customer service, incident resolution, leadership and time management.

I consider myself an analytical, proactive person with a strong desire to continue learning and contributing in the field of cybersecurity and technology consulting.
